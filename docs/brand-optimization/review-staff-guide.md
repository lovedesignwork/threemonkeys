## How to offer it

Offer the card neutrally to all eligible guests who have visited, regardless of whether they appeared happy or raised a concern. Keep it optional. One possible phrase:

> Thank you for visiting Three Monkeys. If you would like to share your experience, this card links to our Google reviews. Honest feedback is welcome, and leaving a review is entirely optional.

Do not request a particular star rating, ask guests to mention a staff name, offer discounts or gifts, pressure guests to review before leaving, or screen guests by satisfaction. Do not ask someone to change or remove a critical review in exchange for assistance. Staff must not write or submit a review on a guest's behalf.

If a guest raises a problem, listen and offer the normal support channel without making help conditional on a review. Do not ask for payment or booking details in a public review. Let guests use their own phone and account; staff should not handle their Google credentials.

This handoff does not authorize automatic email, SMS or WhatsApp review invitations. No outbound invitations were sent. Public review replies remain a separate authorized workflow and should preserve existing staff responses.
