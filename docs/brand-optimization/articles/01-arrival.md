Three Monkeys is inside Hanuman World in Wichit, Phuket. For a first visit, use the restaurant's official location link and tell your driver that you are going to Three Monkeys Restaurant. A recognisable name, the correct pin and your reservation details are more useful than a screenshot of a beautiful table with no address attached.

This guide focuses on getting to the restaurant and coordinating your arrival. If you have not chosen a dining area yet, compare the [seating options](/seats) before finalising the rest of the evening. Your table choice can affect the questions you need to ask about access, timing and the size of your group.

## Save the official destination

The [contact page](/contact) lists the restaurant at 105 Moo 4, Muang Chao Fa Road, Wichit, Mueang Phuket, Phuket 83000, Thailand, inside Hanuman World. Use its Get Directions link to open the official map destination. Keep that link available on your phone before leaving your accommodation.

If someone else is organising transport, send the restaurant name and the location link together. Tell them you are arriving for a meal rather than assuming that every journey to Hanuman World follows the same plan. The site is associated with other activities, so being specific helps separate the dining reservation from any activity booking you may also have.

For guests arriving independently, share one agreed destination. It is easy for a group chat to contain several different screenshots and pins. One person can send a short arrival message with the restaurant name, map link, reservation time and the name used for the table. Keep payment details and private booking information out of public comments.

## Plan the journey around your actual starting point

Do not treat a journey estimate from another guest as your own travel time. Accommodation can be on a different side of Phuket, and the route and road conditions can change. Check the journey from the place you will actually be leaving, then allow room for traffic and weather.

If the meal follows another booking, work backwards from your restaurant time. Include the time needed to finish that activity, collect your belongings and reach the departure point. A full afternoon plan can become uncomfortable when the transfer between two reservations is treated as an afterthought.

When using a driver or taxi, agree where you will be dropped off and how you will recognise the meeting point. For a return pickup, arrange a clear time or a method for contacting the driver. The restaurant's opening hours should not be treated as a promise that any particular transport service will be waiting when you leave.

## Discuss access needs before choosing the table

The restaurant has several dining layouts within its rainforest setting. Photographs show terraces, walkways and different seating structures, but they do not establish a step-free route to every table. If stairs, walking distance, a pushchair or a mobility aid affect your visit, speak to the restaurant before paying for a particular zone.

Describe the requirement in practical terms. For example, explain that a guest needs to avoid steps, needs a short walk from the arrival point, or cannot comfortably use a low seat. Ask which currently available area suits that requirement and where the group should arrive. A general request for a convenient table can mean different things to different people.

The same approach helps when travelling with several generations. Decide who needs an easier route, who will carry any bags and who will speak to the team on arrival. Confirm the arrangement rather than relying on a table photograph to answer those questions.

## Keep the reservation easy to find

Before travelling, check the date, local time, guest count and exact seating or package name in your confirmation. Save it somewhere accessible if your connection is unreliable. If one person paid but another person will arrive first, make sure the arriving guest knows the booking name.

There is a difference between requesting a seat and receiving confirmation of that seat. Keep any agreed changes with the original reservation details. If you have asked for a celebration arrangement, identify one contact person who can discuss it quietly with the team rather than having several guests give different instructions.

If the journey is delayed, contact the restaurant as soon as you know. Explain the reservation time and the revised arrival estimate, then ask what is possible. Do not assume that a later arrival automatically extends the seating period or that another zone can be substituted without checking availability.

## Separate transport, dining and activity inclusions

A table reservation is not automatically a transport booking or an activity ticket. Read the actual inclusions for the product you selected. If you have arranged a package that mentions a transfer, confirm the pickup point, passenger count and applicable service area with the provider.

Likewise, being inside Hanuman World does not establish that a restaurant booking includes an activity. Keep each confirmation and its meeting instructions separate. The [reservation guide](/blog/three-monkeys-phuket-reservations) explains how to distinguish seating deposits, celebration packages and optional extras before you pay.

## Quick arrival questions

### Where can I find the current opening hours?

The contact page currently lists daily opening from 10 AM to 1 AM, with last food orders at midnight. Check directly for a late visit or any special occasion rather than assuming the full menu is available until closing.

### Can I use the same location for the whole group?

Yes: share the official restaurant location link, then agree who will check in and which name the reservation is under. Confirm special drop-off or access arrangements separately.

### Who should I contact if I am delayed?

Use the current details on the [contact page](/contact): +66 98-010-8838 or enjoy@threemonkeysphuket.com. For an imminent arrival issue, a direct call is more useful than leaving an unconfirmed note.

With your route and seating choice clear, [check table availability](/booking). Save the confirmation, share the correct destination and leave enough time to arrive without rushing the start of your meal.
