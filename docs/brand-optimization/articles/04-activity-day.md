Three Monkeys is located inside Hanuman World in Wichit. That makes it useful to consider the restaurant when planning a day in the area, but a shared location does not mean that every restaurant reservation includes an activity, or that every activity ticket includes a Three Monkeys meal.

Start by deciding which parts of the day you want, then check the actual products and confirmations for each. This guide helps you coordinate the dining side without assuming an admission entitlement, fixed activity duration or transfer arrangement that is not listed in your booking.

## Decide whether the meal is the main plan or part of a wider day

If the restaurant is the main reason for your visit, begin with the [seating options](/seats). Choose the layout that suits your group, check the available time and think about the type of meal you want. There is no need to add another activity simply because the restaurant shares a location with one.

If you already have an activity booked, keep its confirmed start time and meeting instructions in front of you when choosing the restaurant reservation. Ask the activity provider about its own schedule and any relevant requirements. Do not use a restaurant article to decide an activity's check-in time, safety conditions or expected finish.

For a group with different plans, agree who is joining which part. Some guests may want to meet for the meal while others have a separate booking earlier in the day. A clear meeting plan is more useful than expecting everyone to follow the same itinerary.

## Check inclusions product by product

Read the actual list of inclusions on the product you are buying. Separate the restaurant table or deposit from food, celebration extras, activities and transport. If a combined offer is available, verify exactly what it includes and any restrictions before comparing it with separate purchases.

A package title is not enough to establish every detail. Check the number of guests covered, the booking date, the applicable time and whether any meal is a set selection or a separate purchase. Ask the provider to clarify anything that affects the total budget or the order of the day.

Keep the confirmations together but clearly labelled. You may need a different reference or check-in instruction for each part. If one person has arranged everything for the group, send each guest the details they need without distributing payment information or other private data unnecessarily.

## Leave a practical gap before the restaurant reservation

Avoid arranging the meal around an unconfirmed finish time. Allow space to complete the earlier booking, gather belongings and reach the restaurant. The exact gap depends on your actual itinerary, so ask the relevant team instead of copying someone else's travel plan.

If your activity provider gives instructions about eating beforehand, follow those instructions when choosing lunch or dinner timing. Three Monkeys can explain its restaurant availability, while the activity provider is the appropriate source for requirements connected with the activity itself.

Think about the group's comfort as well as the timetable. A meal is more enjoyable when everyone has time to arrive and settle in. If people are coming from different places, agree whether the table booking depends on everyone arriving together and ask the restaurant what applies to your reservation.

## Choose the meal independently from the table photograph

Once the timing is workable, use the [food menu](/menu) to plan what you would like to eat. A photograph of a dining zone shows the setting; it does not establish which dishes or drinks are included in a reservation. Read the selected product details and ask about anything unclear.

The menu has several food categories, so a group can discuss preferences before arriving without committing to a rigid order. Note any dietary questions that need an answer in advance. If a requirement determines whether the meal is suitable, resolve it directly with the restaurant before paying.

For a relaxed lunch, you might want to leave the food choice open until everyone is seated. For a celebration, you may have additional arrangements that need to be confirmed earlier. The [reservation guide](/blog/three-monkeys-phuket-reservations) explains the distinction between a seat, a deposit and a package with specific extras.

## Keep transport arrangements explicit

Use the restaurant's [official contact and location page](/contact) when sharing the destination with a driver. Tell them whether you are arriving for the restaurant, an activity or both. The correct place name and the relevant meeting instructions help avoid treating different bookings as interchangeable.

Do not assume that a transfer arranged for one part of the day covers the other. Check the pickup and return times, passenger count and agreed destination. If you want to stay longer for a meal, discuss that with the transport provider before relying on the arrangement.

Guests arriving separately should share one clear meeting plan. Decide who will check in at the restaurant and what name the table is under. If someone is delayed, contact the restaurant promptly rather than assuming the booking time will automatically move with the rest of the itinerary.

## Plan for changes without inventing guarantees

Weather, availability or a change in the group's plans can affect how the day works. Check the terms attached to each booking separately. A change accepted by one provider does not automatically amend another reservation, and a restaurant seating change still depends on the applicable conditions and availability.

If you need help, explain the specific reservation you want to change and the outcome you are requesting. Keep the original confirmation and the response together. Ask for a clear answer before treating a request as settled.

### Does a Three Monkeys table booking include Hanuman World activities?

Only rely on inclusions explicitly listed for the selected product. The location inside Hanuman World does not, by itself, establish an included activity or admission.

### Can I visit the restaurant without organising a full activity day?

The website offers restaurant seat reservations. Use those options to plan the dining visit and contact the team if you have questions about your arrival arrangements.

### What should I book first?

Start with whichever confirmed time is least flexible, then check whether the other part of the day fits. Once the schedule and inclusions are clear, [check Three Monkeys availability](/booking) and keep the dining confirmation with your plan.
