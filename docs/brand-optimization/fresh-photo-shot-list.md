## Capture and delivery

- Deliver original camera files before cropping or compressing. Use daylight or accurate ambient light; no filters that materially change food colour, portions or the venue.
- For each distinct scene, take a landscape frame with room around the subject. A portrait alternative may be useful, but it is still the same scene and must not be treated as a separate unused photograph.
- For food, record the exact current menu name and ask the team to confirm availability. Do not infer ingredients, allergens or certification from appearance.
- Avoid identifiable guests unless appropriate permission has been obtained. Ask staff before featuring them. Keep receipts, booking screens, payment terminals, number plates and guest names out of frame.
- Record the capture date, photographer, venue area, dish name where relevant, permission status and intended editorial subject. Use a descriptive filename such as `2026-11-10-three-monkeys-east-entrance-daylight-01.jpg`; confirm the actual area name rather than adopting this example.
- Supply several original options for each scene. The editor selects one and records its SHA-256, visual fingerprint, full source filename, caption, alt text and exclusive article/post/profile assignment. Crops, recolours or near-identical burst frames do not qualify as distinct assets.
- Keep original files. Produce delivery-sized WebP article variants and JPEG GBP uploads only after selection. Inspect every exported image at its displayed size, including the mobile crop. Retain source and exported hashes in the ledger.

## Facts the photographer can help verify

Record rather than guess: the real entrance sign and route, step-free alternatives if staff confirms them, current seating zone names and table setup, actual menu names, and which celebration elements are standard versus optional. Photographs alone cannot establish opening hours, accessibility, capacity limits, allergy safety or included services; ask the responsible staff member and record their confirmation separately.
