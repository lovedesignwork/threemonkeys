To make a Three Monkeys Phuket reservation, choose the seating or package that matches your visit, select the available date and time, enter the correct guest details and review the order before payment. Read the conditions attached to the selected product. A seating deposit, a celebration package and an optional extra are different purchases, even when they all relate to the same evening.

The booking flow should be your reference for current availability and the amount payable. Articles and saved screenshots can help you plan, but they should not override the live selection. If a detail is essential, such as a particular seat or an access requirement, confirm it with the restaurant before completing the transaction.

## Step one: choose what you are reserving

Start with the [seating page](/seats) if you mainly want a table. The available products describe different areas, including signature seats and other indoor or outdoor options. Read the name and description carefully rather than selecting only by the first photograph you recognise.

Start with [special packages](/special-packages) if the main purpose is a birthday, romantic occasion or proposal with arranged extras. Review the inclusions, setup location and conditions. A package can contain specific celebration services while leaving the meal to be purchased separately. Its title alone is not enough to establish what is included.

If you are deciding between signature seats, the [Monkey Dome versus Monkey Nest guide](/blog/monkey-dome-vs-monkey-nest) can help narrow the choice. Once you have selected a product, check that its capacity and arrangement suit the whole group. Do not assume a general note requesting another zone changes the product being purchased.

## Step two: understand the pricing unit and deposit

Look at whether the displayed amount is per person, per table or a package total. Multiplying the wrong unit is an easy way to misunderstand the cost of a group booking. Review the order summary with the actual guest count before comparing it with another option.

Some seating products describe their deposit as redeemable, with conditions about unused credit. Read those conditions for your selected seat. A redeemable deposit is not automatically the same as a refundable payment, and neither phrase means that food is unlimited. Ask how the credit is applied if anything is unclear.

For a celebration package, separate the arranged extras from the meal budget. Check any taxes, service charges and optional additions shown in the applicable details or checkout. The useful question is not only what you pay now, but what you may need to pay during the visit.

| Celebration package | Listed inclusions, exclusions and setup area |
| Optional extra | Quantity, price and whether it needs prior confirmation |

## Step three: prepare the right guest details

Use an email address and telephone number you can access while travelling. Check spelling and the country code before submitting. The booking name should be recognisable to the person arriving at the restaurant, especially if you are arranging the meal on someone else's behalf.

Give the correct party size and explain relevant details about children or seating needs. For a celebration, keep the request clear: the occasion, any agreed cake wording and the contact person coordinating it. Booking notes are useful for communicating a request, but they should not be treated as confirmation that an unlisted service has been accepted.

Tell the team about an access requirement before paying if it determines which area will work. Likewise, discuss serious dietary restrictions directly rather than assuming that a note alone settles the question. The restaurant can explain what it can accommodate for the actual reservation.

## Step four: review before payment

Pause at the order summary and read it as if you were checking someone else's booking. Confirm the date, local time, number of guests, product name and selected extras. If the total is not what you expected, resolve the difference before proceeding instead of assuming it will be corrected on arrival.

Read the applicable cancellation and amendment conditions. They can differ between a seating reservation and a special package. A general FAQ should not be used to override the terms attached to the purchase. Save the relevant details with your confirmation so that you can refer to them if plans change.

After payment, keep the confirmation or booking reference. If you are uncertain whether a transaction completed, check for confirmation and contact the restaurant before making another payment. Avoid creating a second reservation simply because an email has not yet appeared in the inbox.

## Step five: organise arrival and your return

Save the official location for Three Monkeys inside Hanuman World in Wichit. Share that pin with your driver and with anyone arriving separately. Allow for traffic and weather rather than treating a map estimate as an exact journey duration.

If a package includes transport, confirm the pickup location, passenger count and service area. Do not assume every reservation includes a transfer, or that a listed transfer covers an airport journey. For an ordinary seat booking, plan transport independently unless another arrangement has been explicitly confirmed.

Keep a return plan as well. Knowing how you will get back lets you enjoy the meal without trying to organise the whole journey at the end. Check any seating duration when deciding how much time to leave for dinner and a celebration.

## If your plans change

Contact the restaurant as soon as you know that the headcount, timing or required arrangements may change. Include the booking reference and the specific change you are requesting. A move to another zone depends on availability and the conditions of the original purchase.

If rain is forecast and you have selected an outdoor area, ask what applies to that reservation. Do not assume an automatic move to an indoor table, a refund or an alternative setup. Getting the answer before travelling makes the next decision much easier.

Use the [contact page](/contact) for the restaurant's current communication channels. Keep payment information and personal details out of public social comments; provide the booking reference through the agreed direct channel instead.

## Reservation questions

### Can I walk in?

If you want a particular signature seat, booking ahead is the clearer way to check availability. For walk-in arrangements, contact the restaurant rather than assuming that a desired zone will be free.

### Does paying a deposit include a fixed menu?

Not necessarily. Read the selected seating product and its redemption conditions. A seat deposit and a fixed meal are different things.

### Will a booking note guarantee my request?

No. Use notes to communicate, then seek explicit confirmation for anything essential or outside the listed inclusions.

With the product and conditions clear, [start your reservation](/booking). Keep the confirmation accessible and arrive knowing which part of the evening has already been arranged.
