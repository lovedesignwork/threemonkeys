A romantic dinner in Phuket works best when the setting and pace match the people sharing it. At Three Monkeys, the appeal is dining among trees, with a choice of seating areas and the option to arrange a celebration. Start with the table and the kind of evening you want. Then decide whether a regular reservation is enough or whether a named package includes details you would value.

There is no need to turn every dinner for two into an elaborate production. A comfortable seat, dishes you both enjoy and enough time to talk can be the whole plan. For an anniversary or honeymoon, additional arrangements may be worthwhile, but they should be chosen deliberately rather than assumed to come with a reservation.

## Decide what romantic means for your evening

Some couples enjoy a lively restaurant and the energy around them. Others want a more contained seating arrangement and an unhurried conversation. Before comparing photographs, agree on the qualities that matter most: the surroundings, the food, a special decoration or simply spending time together without another appointment immediately afterwards.

Three Monkeys is a rainforest restaurant in Wichit. Its greenery offers a different setting from a beach dinner. Do not plan around an unobstructed sunset or a sea view unless the restaurant has confirmed that for the particular seat. The strongest plan is one that still feels enjoyable if the light, weather or surrounding activity differs from a favourite photograph.

## Choose the seating before choosing the extras

The [Monkey Dome](/packages/monkey-dome) has a rounded, more enclosed form, while the [Monkey Nest](/packages/monkey-nest) uses curved open seating among the trees. Both are distinctive, but the architecture alone does not tell you which will feel more comfortable. Look at the table arrangement, the way you sit and the access route as well as the exterior.

Our [Dome versus Nest guide](/blog/monkey-dome-vs-monkey-nest) compares the practical differences. You can also consider the other areas in the [seating collection](/seats). Ask for advice if you want conventional dining chairs, easier access or a particular relationship to the surrounding atmosphere. A request for privacy should be discussed specifically; it does not automatically mean exclusive use of an entire area.

## A table reservation and a celebration package are different

A seating reservation secures the product you select under its booking conditions. It is not automatically a decorated dinner package, and a deposit should not be confused with a fixed food menu. Read the details beside the actual product before comparing costs.

The [Ultimate Dinner Package](/packages/ultimate-dinner) is presented as a romantic celebration arrangement. Its listed features include a table setup, flowers and other specified extras. Crucially, the package information says that food and drinks are purchased separately, alongside the particular beverage provision described in its inclusions. Do not interpret the package name as an all-inclusive meal.

Use the current package page and checkout to confirm the total, the assigned zone, the advance-booking requirement and cancellation conditions. If you want a different seat, a non-alcoholic option or an adjustment to the arrangement, get an answer before paying. A request written in notes is not the same as acceptance of a change.

## Order a meal you can enjoy together

The most useful menu conversation is about preferences, not impressiveness. Decide whether you want to share several dishes or each choose a main. If one person enjoys more heat, ask which preparations can be adjusted and whether a spicy dish can be kept separate. Do not assume every sauce or garnish is interchangeable.

A shared meal can move through contrasting flavours: something fresh, a richer dish and rice or another accompaniment. Leave room to decide about dessert after the main part of the meal. Ordering everything at once may suit some diners; others will prefer to ask about pacing. Either way, tell the team what kind of evening you have in mind.

Browse the [menu](/menu) together, or use our [what-to-order guide](/blog/authentic-southern-thai-food) to narrow the choices. Mention allergies directly when ordering, even if they were also included in your reservation. A special occasion does not make dietary details less important.

## Leave breathing room in the schedule

Build in time for the journey to Wichit and for finding your table. Arriving directly from a tightly scheduled activity can leave little room for traffic, weather or a change of clothes. Check the seating duration associated with your booking rather than planning an unrestricted evening in a timed zone.

If photographs matter to you, decide whether you prefer daylight or the illuminated evening setting. Take a few photographs before settling into the meal, without turning the whole visit into a photo session. Ask permission before including other guests, and keep shared paths clear. If professional photography is part of a package, confirm what the service includes and how it is arranged.

## Make a surprise manageable

Give the team a clear description of the occasion and one reliable contact person. Explain any surprise that must remain confidential, and agree on the communication method. Details such as the timing of flowers, a cake message or a particular table setup need explicit confirmation; they should not be inferred from an example photograph.

For an occasion with a fixed moment, such as a proposal, contact the restaurant before making other commitments. The restaurant has a [proposal package](/packages/will-you-marry-me), whose arrangements should be reviewed separately. Avoid assuming that an ordinary romantic dinner reservation includes proposal coordination.

## Questions couples often ask

### Does reserving a romantic seat include decorations?

Check the named product. Seating reservations and celebration packages have different inclusions. Confirm any specific setup you want before paying.

### Is dinner included in the Ultimate Dinner Package?

The package page states that food and drinks are purchased separately, with its specified beverage inclusion listed separately. Read the current inclusions and budget for the meal accordingly.

### What should we do if rain is forecast?

Contact reservations about your chosen zone and ask what arrangements apply. Do not assume an outdoor booking can automatically move to another seat.

Choose the evening that suits you, then [check availability](/booking). The useful details are the ones that let you arrive knowing what has been arranged and enjoy the person across the table.
